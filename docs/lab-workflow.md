# Lab Workflow

## Environment
A local Zammad helpdesk instance used for hands-on IT service desk practice.

## Roles
- **Admin:** Abdulaziz Alenazi
- **Agent:** Ahmed Technician
- **Customer:** Mohammed Employee
- **Group:** IT Helpdesk

## Incident Simulation
- **Ticket:** #14003 — Unable to Connect to Wi-Fi
- **Customer:** Mohammed Employee
- **Owner:** Ahmed Technician
- **Priority:** 2 Normal
- **Status:** Open

### Steps
1. Created a sample customer incident.
2. Assigned the incident to the technician.
3. Sent a public technician reply asking the customer to restart the laptop and reconnect.
4. Signed in as the customer and replied that the problem remained unresolved.
5. Confirmed the customer reply appeared in the ticket conversation.
6. Kept the ticket open for further investigation.

## Learning Outcomes
Practiced basic service desk ticket ownership, role separation, communication and lifecycle tracking.

## Scope Note
The incident is fictional and was not technically resolved in the lab.
